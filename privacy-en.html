Privacy Policy

Хамелеон · CKO Gastronomie GmbH · Effective October 16, 2026

РусскийEnglish

Хамелеон is a game with offline core gameplay and no advertising, purchases, accounts, analytics or tracking. Game data and Workshop materials are processed and stored on your device; the app does not send them to the publisher. You choose whether to use photos, the microphone, location, reminders or link sharing. System services, recipients you choose, visits to the website and voluntary support correspondence are described separately below.

Information we collect

The game's publisher is CKO Gastronomie GmbH. The game has no registration, authentication, advertising or analytics SDKs, server of its own, or direct HTTP API requests. We do not receive your progress, photos, audio, location or tracking identifiers from the app. The app's privacy manifest declares NSPrivacyTracking = false and an empty NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes list. Its use of UserDefaults for local progress and settings is declared with reason CA92.1.

If you voluntarily email kej3zdzn@cloud-temp.com, the publisher receives your sender address, message and any attachments you choose to send. This correspondence is used to respond to your request. Please do not include unnecessary personal information. The absence of data collection from the game does not mean that we receive no information through such emails.

Information stored on your device

  • Progress and settings. JSON Data is stored in UserDefaults under progress.v1 and settings.v1: records for the 30 campaign levels, daily route records for up to the 60 most recently recorded dates, the climbing height record, seen hints, sound, music and haptics settings, and the reminder setting and time. The limit of 60 recorded dates is not automatic deletion after 60 days.
  • Route library. Library/Application Support/Routes/routes.json stores each route's UUID and creation date, origin and code, record and fireflies, the rounded placeKey and label for a place-based route, a photo flag and two palette colours. A tune-based route also retains MIDI note fields. The game sets no overall limit on the number of saved routes and no automatic expiry period for them.
  • Photo copies. A JPEG copy of the camera image or selected photo is saved in Library/Application Support/Routes/Photos/, with a filename matching the route's UUID. Its longest side is limited to 1200 pixels, at JPEG quality 0.82. This stored copy is not blurred: blur is applied only when displaying the game background. It is not a temporary background; the copy stays with the saved route until deletion. The camera does not automatically save the original shot to the system Photos library.
  • Forest sound cache. Two locally synthesised WAV files for Night jungle and Morning canopy are created in Caches: each is 60 seconds, mono, 16-bit PCM at 44.1 kHz. They are not microphone recordings. iOS may clear the cache; the app can recreate it.

The game has no cloud synchronisation of its own. However, the app does not exclude Application Support files or preferences from system backups: depending on your settings, they may be included in a device backup, including iCloud. This is a separate iOS feature, not a transfer to the publisher's server.

Device permissions and system capabilities

Access is requested when you start the related feature. Denial, cancellation or unavailability affects that feature, not core gameplay. Incoming links and background playback do not have a separate permission prompt. Most relevant screens let you open iOS Settings; after changing access, some screens need to be reopened. Automatic recovery on returning is not guaranteed.

  • Notifications. Daily route reminders are initially off. If you enable them and choose a time, the game requests permission and schedules one-off local notifications for the next seven calendar days, skipping daily routes you have already completed. The schedule is refreshed when you open the game and when relevant changes occur. This is not an indefinitely repeating notification; without another refresh it does not extend beyond that window. No delivery server is used. If permission is denied, reminders are unavailable; you can still open Daily route yourself.
  • Incoming links. chamaeleo://daily, chamaeleo://level/1 and links that begin with chamaeleo://route/ followed by a route code open a daily route, level or shared route locally, without a server request. The game must be installed to handle them. If a link or its code is not recognised, that route cannot be opened from the link; normal gameplay does not depend on it.
  • Background audio. Forest sounds offers Night jungle and Morning canopy, two locally synthesised loops. They can play in the background and with the screen locked, with play and pause controls on the Lock Screen and a 15-, 30- or 60-minute sleep timer. No microphone is used for this feature. Playback is optional and can be paused; unavailable audio does not block the game.
  • Camera. Workshop → Take a photo requests camera access to make a route from bright areas of the frame. The JPEG copy described above is saved, not an automatic original in Photos. If access is denied, you cancel, or the camera is unavailable, no new route is created this way; you can keep playing or use another Workshop method.
  • Microphone. Workshop → Hum a tune → Record a tune records up to eight seconds to identify note pitches. Short PCM windows are processed in RAM; raw audio and vocal timbre are not written to a file, and no cloud speech recognition is used. At least four recognised notes are needed; at most 40 are used. MIDI fields are retained in the derived route, while note durations indirectly affect its geometry. Denial, cancellation, an unavailable microphone or too few notes prevents creating a tune-based route, not core gameplay.
  • Photo library reading. Workshop → From library requests .readWrite access and also supports .limited access. With limited access, only permitted images are visible; Select more lets you expand the selection. The selected image is used to build a route and save a JPEG copy. iOS is allowed to download both previews and full photos from iCloud; an image not yet downloaded may need internet access. Progress, Cancel and an error message, including for offline failure, are provided. Denial makes import unavailable; cancellation or an error does not prevent core gameplay.
  • Saving to Photos. After completing a route successfully, or losing a Rising water attempt, Save postcard requests .addOnly access and saves a 1080 × 1350 pixel postcard to the photo library. This is a separate action, not automatic saving of camera shots. The postcard's contents are described below. If permission is denied or saving fails, the postcard is not saved; your game result is unchanged.
  • Location while using the app. Workshop → This place → Find this place's route requests LocationWhenInUse access and a one-time location fix with a 20-second timeout. Coordinates are rounded to a 0.1° cell; the biome and geometry are selected locally, and the route retains a rounded place key and label. The app does not look up a city, use CLGeocoder or MapKit, or send the original precise coordinates outside the app. The system location fix itself may use network access and Apple services. Denial, unavailability or timeout prevents creating a place-based route; other modes remain available.

Links, postcards and sharing at your choice

The system ShareLink sheet opens at your request. A link with the chamaeleo://route/ prefix contains a code with the route's geometry, theme, colours, obstacles, movement parameters and MIDI notes. Raw JPEGs, PCM, placeKey and explicit coordinates are not included in that code. However, it contains data derived from the source material: omitting the original does not guarantee anonymity. Sharing from a result screen also adds the attempt time to the shared text.

A 1080 × 1350 postcard contains a diagram, trajectory, route name, date, time or height, and fireflies. It does not contain the original photo or audio. A place-based route's name includes rounded coordinates, so they may appear on the postcard. The game only saves postcards to Photos: it has no separate postcard image-sharing or Files export action. After saving, you may share the postcard from Photos or make other copies using system apps yourself.

You choose the recipient or service used to share a link. Further processing, storage and forwarding depend on that recipient or service; transmission may require a network connection. Deleting a route in the game does not revoke links or copies already shared.

Network requests and third parties

Core gameplay, local route creation, incoming link parsing, forest sound synthesis and local reminders do not require a publisher server. This does not mean every iOS action is exclusively local: iCloud photo downloads, system assistance with a location fix, backups and sharing you choose may use networks and third-party services. iCloud features and Apple Account sign-in are handled by the operating system; the game does not receive their authentication tokens. Local photo use and location fixes do not require an Apple Account.

Opening the policy in the in-app SFSafariViewController browser loads a website hosted on GitHub Pages and requires internet access. GitHub receives standard technical request information, including your IP address and browser information, under its Privacy Statement. This website has no analytics of its own, advertising trackers or data collection forms. Opening documents and using email are separate network actions; their unavailability does not block the game.

Children

The game has no accounts, advertising or analytics. If a child uses optional features, parents or legal guardians should supervise access to photos, the microphone and location, postcard saving and link sharing. If you believe a child sent us personal information by email, please contact support so that we can consider its deletion.

Your rights and deletion

You can manage permissions in iOS Settings, turn off reminders and stop forest sounds. Workshop → My routes → More → Delete removes the route entry and its record, and the app also requests deletion of the associated JPEG. File operation errors are not reported separately: complete file deletion cannot be guaranteed if a disk operation fails. Deleting one route does not clear overall progress, settings or the sound cache.

Fully uninstalling the app removes its local container, but not source photos, postcards saved to Photos, links held by recipients, copies in Files or other apps, or system backups. Manage those copies separately through the relevant system or service. We have no remote access to your container or backups.

For access, correction or deletion of information you voluntarily sent to support, or questions about your applicable rights, email kej3zdzn@cloud-temp.com. Do not send photos or location information unless they are needed for your request.
