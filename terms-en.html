Modes and optional features

Gameplay includes the campaign, Daily route, endless Rising water and Workshop. The daily route is selected using your device's date. Core gameplay works offline. Optional features — routes from photos, tunes or places, reminders, forest sounds, incoming links, postcard saving and link sharing — are used at your choice. Denial of permission, cancellation or feature unavailability does not block core gameplay.

Reminders are initially off. When enabled, the game schedules one-off notifications for the next seven calendar days, skipping daily routes already completed; the schedule is refreshed when you open the game and when relevant changes occur. There is no indefinitely repeating reminder. Incoming links are handled locally without a server. Forest sounds offers two synthesised loops with Lock Screen controls and a 15-, 30- or 60-minute sleep timer.

Your content

Rights to your photos, tunes and other source materials remain with you or their respective rights holders. Use materials for which you have the necessary rights and consents, especially when they concern other people. Creating a route does not transfer ownership of your source materials to the publisher.

Workshop processes materials on your device. A photo copy is saved with the route as a JPEG and is not blurred in storage; only the game background is blurred. Microphone recording lasts up to eight seconds, is processed in short windows in RAM, and is not saved as a raw audio file or a recording of vocal timbre. The route retains MIDI note fields and derived geometry. A place-based route stores a rounded 0.1° cell key and label; the app does not transmit the original precise coordinates outside the app.

When you share through the system ShareLink sheet, the link code contains derived route data: geometry, theme, colours, obstacles, movement and MIDI notes, but not the source JPEG, PCM, place key or explicit coordinates. Text shared from a result screen also includes the attempt time. This is not a guarantee of anonymity. A postcard is saved only to Photos and includes a diagram, trajectory, name, date, time or height, and fireflies, but not the source photo or audio. A place-based route's name can reveal rounded coordinates on the postcard. The game has no separate postcard image-sharing or Files export action; later sharing or copying through other apps is your choice.

You choose whom to send links to and what to do with saved postcards. Recipients and chosen services may store or forward these materials. Deleting a route through Workshop → My routes → More → Delete removes its entry and record and requests JPEG deletion; complete file deletion is not guaranteed if disk operations fail. This action does not clear overall progress, settings or the cache, or revoke shared copies. Fully uninstalling the app removes its container, but not source photos, postcards in Photos, copies in Files or other apps, recipients' links or system backups. There is no cloud synchronisation of the app's own; system backups may include game data, including in iCloud.
